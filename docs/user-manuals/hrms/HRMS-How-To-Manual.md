A simple guide for HR team members. Follow the left menu under **HR**.

**Not covered in this guide:** Performance, Revised salary, Incentives, and any AI / resume-auto features.

## Before you begin

1. Sign in to iConnect Plus with your HR login.
2. From the main menu, open **HR**.
3. If your company has more than one legal entity, pick the right one from the **company switcher** at the top of the screen. Lists and payroll follow that choice.
4. Best order for a new company: finish **Org Setup** first, then hire people, then run payroll.

### Menu map (left sidebar)

## 1. Dashboard

**Purpose:** One place to see headcount, leave today, open roles, people in onboarding, today’s attendance, upcoming birthdays / anniversaries, holidays, and charts.

### Steps

1. Click **Dashboard & Reports** in the left menu.
2. Read the greeting and the four **Key Metrics** cards at the top.
3. Click a metric card if you want to jump to that area (for example, open roles opens recruitment-related work).
4. Check **Upcoming Events** (birthdays / work anniversaries) and **Holiday Calendar**.
5. Check **Today’s Attendance** (Present / Absent / On Leave).
6. Use **Quick Actions** for common shortcuts.
7. Under **HR Analytics**, choose the time range (This month / Last 3 months / Last 6 months) and review the charts.
8. Click **Refresh** if you just hired people or synced attendance and the numbers look old.

## 3. Onboarding

**Purpose:** Guide a selected candidate from “offer accepted” to “active employee”.

### Steps

1. Open **Onboarding**.
2. Create a new onboarding case (or continue one that came from recruitment).
3. Enter candidate email and joining details carefully — the invite goes to that email.
4. Click **Send invitation**.
5. Candidate opens the mail, follows the link, and signs in with the access details provided.
6. Candidate completes the portal steps (personal information, bank details, company policies with signature, photo where asked).
7. Watch the status on the onboarding list (invitation sent → in progress → submitted → HR review → joined).
8. If the candidate did not get the mail, use **Resend invitation**.
9. When everything is complete and verified, **activate / mark as joined** so they appear in the employee list.

## 5. Payroll (through Payslip)

Do these four steps in order. Do not skip “Assign salary” before running the month.

```
Salary structure → Assign salary → Run payroll → Payslip
```

### 5.1 Salary structure

**Purpose:** Create reusable salary templates (CTC breakup).

1. Open **Payroll → Salary structure**.
2. Click create / add structure.
3. Enter structure name and CTC / component breakup as your finance policy defines.
4. Save the template.
5. Create more structures if you have different bands (for example junior vs senior).

1. Open **Payroll → Assign salary**.
2. Select the employee.
3. Select the salary structure.
4. Confirm monthly / CTC values.
5. Save.

## 6. EDoc (employee documents)

**Purpose:** Store employee files, define document types, and manage onboarding policies / company documents.

### Tabs you will see

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| Employees | One employee’s document folder (vault) |
| Document Types | Master list of document names (Aadhaar, PAN, offer, etc.) |

### Steps

1. Open **EDoc**.
2. First time setup: create **Document Types** your company needs.
3. Upload **Onboarding Policies** that candidates must accept during onboarding.
4. Open **Employees** tab, pick a person, and review or add files.
5. Use **Other Documents** for general policies or notices, and send them when required.
6. Prefer **Preview** to open a file; download only when necessary.

## 9. Offboarding

**Purpose:** Run a clean exit — approvals, notice, clearance, documents, and final settlement.

### Big picture

1. Start an offboarding case  
2. Approvals (Manager → IT → Accounts → HR)  
3. Notice period or direct exit  
4. Clearance / agreements  
5. Exit interview and exit letters  
6. Final settlement (FNF) and close  

### Steps

1. Open **Offboarding**.
2. Click to **start offboarding** for the employee.
3. Choose separation type (resignation, retirement, or other options shown).
4. Enter resignation / exit date, notice days (if applicable), expected last working day, and reason.
5. Submit and track approvals in order.
6. Complete clearance items and any NOC / confidentiality forms when the screen asks.
7. Record the exit interview.
8. Attach exit documents (resignation letter, relieving letter, experience letter, etc.).
9. Complete final settlement when finance / HR confirm dues are clear.
10. After close, the person should show under ex-employees in the Workforce list.

## 11. Common problems (plain language)

| What you see | What to try |
|--------------|-------------|
| Wrong people or empty list | Change the company switcher at the top to the correct entity |
| Candidate / joiner did not get email | Confirm the email address, resend invitation, ask them to check spam |
| Cannot run payroll for someone | Assign salary to that employee first |
| Payslip shows wrong company name / logo | Fix the entity details under Org Setup → Organisation |
| Org chart looks incomplete | Update reporting managers and people roles in Org Setup |
| Attendance missing punches | Check biometric device setup under IT & Admin |
| Offboarding not visible | Use **Offboarding** in the HR left menu |
