Introducing Avenna FlowSync

Nov 22, 2025

5 min read

What’s new?

The AI Insights Module delivers real-time, automated analysis of your business data, uncovering patterns, anomalies, and opportunities for growth. Powered by artificial intelligence, it interprets data, generates forecasts, and provides actionable insights through intuitive dashboards — helping teams make smarter, faster decisions without manual reporting.

Workflow Templates make process automation effortless. With a library of ready-to-use scenarios for common business tasks, you can launch automations in minutes and easily adapt them to fit your organization’s specific needs. It’s the quickest way to streamline operations and scale efficiency without complex setup.  

Data Export 2.0 simplifies how teams share analytics and reports. Export in one click to formats like PDF, Excel, or CSV, or share secure links directly from the platform. With faster delivery and access control, collaboration becomes seamless while maintaining full data security.

Feature Updates

Advanced Integrations: Seamlessly connect Avenna with ERP, CRM, and analytics.
Real-Time Analytics Sync: Instantly update dashboards to keep all metrics aligned

Fixes & Improvements

Fixed data refresh delays in live reporting modules - Live dashboards now update instantly, eliminating previous data lag and ensuring that all metrics reflect the latest information in real time.

Improved chart rendering speed and responsiveness - Visualizations now load faster and respond smoothly to user interactions, delivering a more fluid and efficient analytics experience. 

Resolved minor layout inconsistencies on smaller screens - Interface elements have been fine-tuned for mobile and tablet displays, ensuring clean alignment and a consistent look across all devices. 

Enhanced automation accuracy in workflow triggers - Workflow automations now run with greater precision and reliability, reducing false triggers and ensuring consistent task execution. 

Improved accessibility and keyboard navigation support - The platform is now easier to navigate using only a keyboard, providing a more inclusive and accessible experience for all users.
