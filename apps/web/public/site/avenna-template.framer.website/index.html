FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Get answers to common questions here.

How does iConnect Plus automate workflows?

State machines and gates encode your operating system. Approvals, notifications, and AI suggestions run on the same blueprint - not a bolted-on chatbot.

Can it connect with tools we already use?

Yes. Modules share one tenant graph and integrate with identity (Microsoft Entra), email, and your existing finance / CRM / HR processes without forcing a rip-and-replace.

Is our data secure?

Tenant and company isolation, RBAC, soft-delete, and versioned records are defaults. Only authorized roles see what they are scoped to.

How fast can we go live?

Discovery workshops map your real process, then we configure blueprints and roll out module by module - starting with the path that unlocks the most value.

Do teams need deep tech skills?

Operators work in purpose-built screens. Admins configure roles and modules. Custom modules are engineered with you - not left as a vendor backlog.
