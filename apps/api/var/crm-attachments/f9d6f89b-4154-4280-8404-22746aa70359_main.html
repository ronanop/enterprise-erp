About Sam's Fitness

More than a gym — it's a lifestyle

Built for Every Body

Founded by Sam Mitchell — a certified personal trainer with over 15 years of experience — Sam's Fitness has been the heart of our community since 2010. We believe fitness should be accessible, enjoyable, and results-driven.

Our 10,000 sq ft facility features free weights, cardio zones, group classes, a dedicated yoga studio, and recovery areas. Whether you want to lose weight, build muscle, or simply feel better, our team is here for you every step of the way.
